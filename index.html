04 / AKAORA MEET EXPLAINED

An online meeting website, without the login.

Akaora Meet is a browser-based video meeting app by Akaora Technologies. Start a new meeting, share the room code or invite link, and let others request to join. No account registration is required.

Can I use Akaora Meet without signing up?

Yes. Enter a display name to create a room or join one using a room code or an invite link. No account signup is required.

Can I share my screen and chat during a meeting?

Yes. The meeting toolbar supports screen sharing and in-meeting chat, alongside microphone and camera controls.

How do I invite someone to a meeting?

Copy your meeting code or share the invite link. The host can review join requests before admitting participants.

Does the meeting website work on phones?

Akaora Meet works in supported modern browsers on desktop and mobile. Camera, microphone and screen-sharing availability depend on the browser and device permissions.
